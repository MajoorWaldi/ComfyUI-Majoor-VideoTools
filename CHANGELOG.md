# Changelog

## Unreleased

### In short

- **Three ready workflows** in `workflows/` (also in `recipes/`): `SECATOR Remix` (a video is the template of the edit, your subject and sound replace it), `SECATOR Creative Text` (an idea, a soundtrack made in a mood, an edit cut on its beats) and `SECATOR Creative Music` (your music, an edit built on it). Sections CONTROLS, INPUTS (up to 5 pictures), MODELS, the pipeline, the shot block, assembly; sub-graphs for the technical steps; GUIDE, DEPENDENCIES and WIRING notes.
- **New nodes and inputs**: Edit Skills (skills with modules, profiles, pictures / videos / voice / dialogue / plate), Edit Mode, Get Video Components Advanced (`has_audio`), Select Every N Frames, Load Video Advanced, Audio Rhythm, Music Brief, Cut Markers, Deploy Shots, Compare Video, Preview Video; Scene Detect & Split `energy`; Select Shot `location`; Shot Assemble `replace_source_audio` and `voice_shots`.
- **Skills** instead of pasted prompts: `timeline_planner`, `dynamic_edit_skill`, `h3_shot_prompt_from_plan`, `remix_shot_prompt`, `shot_reader`, `clip_judge`, `prompt_enhancer`, `music_brief_*`, seven editing profiles, two modes. One skill per LLM call; `<Picture 1>` is always the subject to preserve.
- **Energy of a video** (flashes, shakes, punch-ins, whips, push) measured and put back on the generated takes; **location plates** (one empty plate per place, shared by its shots); **voice and dialogue**; **quality check** (a judge and a second take); **trick** of a reference video.
- **Local models only**: Gemma 4 (12B and E4B) as the LLM through *Generate Text*, MiniMax H3, ACE-Step 1.5 (the music; MiniMax Music 3 was the first engine and the workflows no longer use it), FLUX.2 klein 4B, BiRefNet (the subject mask). No Ollama, no network access from the pack.
- **Removed**: the skills `remix_edit_director`, `remix_planner`, `remix_shot_analyzer`, the recipes `minimax_h3_ref2va` and `minimax_h3_ref2va_plan` (kept only as test fixtures), the example prompts of the old analyzer and director, the Ollama route, the five modes and the presets of Edit Mode (two modes now), the mask of the remix.
- **Fixed in the audit of the docs and the tests**: the `README` is rewritten (it still said four nodes, no workflow, never run in ComfyUI); the model links were checked against the repo listings (the plates had a wrong repo); `test_pipeline.py` is skipped when `scenedetect` is missing instead of erroring; a test of `plan_from_video` that depended on the order of the tests (a real module already imported wins over `sys.modules`) now patches the package attribute; the copies of three skills in `examples/prompts/` are what Edit Skills gives with its default settings (the h3 copy had both the dialogue and the no-dialogue rules).

### Details, newest first

- Consolidation: ACE-Step replaces MiniMax Music 3 in `SECATOR Remix` and `SECATOR Creative Text` (the `... ACE` copies and the MiniMax versions are gone: three workflows remain), and `SECATOR Creative Music` cuts your music with Music Fit (a MUSIC START control, -1 = the strongest part) instead of its first seconds.
- Switches: the workflows have EFFECTS (Shot Assemble `effects`), TEXT (Edit Skills `text_overlays` and the new `text` input of Shot Assemble, which keeps the other effects and drops the text of the plan) and MOTION GRAPHICS ON (a lazy switch: the node and the mask do not run when it is off; Remix: EXTRA MOTION GRAPHICS ON). Test: a fourth export without the text in `test_timeline.py`.
- Format and quality: Create Timeline and Edit Skills get optional `width` and `height` (the plan JSON may carry `width` and `height` too: they replace `aspect_ratio` and `resolution`; even sizes, 64 to 4096); the Creative workflows have a FORMAT AND QUALITY control (ComfyUI's Resolution Selector, multiple of 32, 0.35 MP by default) wired to both, and the H3 sub-graph has a `megapixels` input: its size is the size of the shot, at most that many megapixels (0.98 = H3's 768 x 1344), rounded to 32. Remix gets a QUALITY control for that cap. Measured on a 24 GB GPU (one 5.2 s shot, 8 steps): 768 x 1344 samples in 82 s (decode 7 s) against about 21 s (decode 2 s) at 432 x 768, about 112 s a shot against 48 s, with no memory error. The sizes of the Creative edits were 432 x 768 before: that is, every Creative edit was generated at 432 x 768 (a third of H3's pixels) and Remix at the size of its source, whatever it was. Tests: a size in pixels in `test_timeline.py`.
- Music: **Music Fit** (`MajoorMusicFit`, `core/music_fit.py`) cuts a long music to the length of the edit on its bars (downbeat = the beat whose bass onsets are strongest, window = the loudest one with a bonus for a drop, looped by whole bars when too short, a 5 ms fade in, 80 ms fade out, `start` snaps to a bar). The beat phase of `detect_beats` now also follows the bass band (linear flux below 200 Hz, weight 2): on an ACE-Step track the cuts were 0.2 s before the kick (the hats pulled the phase), now within 0.02 s. New workflows `SECATOR Creative Text ACE` and `SECATOR Remix ACE` (ACE-Step 1.5 turbo: tags, BPM, a track of length + 80 s because it stops early on short ones, then Music Fit). Tests: `test_music_fit.py`, a kick-versus-hats phase test, a Music Fit check in `check_nodes`. Measured on a free GPU (Gemma 4, H3 turbo, 8 steps): plan 40 s, music about 19 s, per shot about 45 s (H3 prompt 9.5, pose 3.5, generation 26, compare 4.4).
- Motion Graphics, more layers: `outline` (a contour around the subject: solid, double, dashed, neon, glow), `effect` (chromatic aberration, glitch, grid, dots, scanlines; on the frame, the subject or the background; flicker), `emoji` (color emoji font: Segoe UI Emoji, Noto, Apple, or `MAJOOR_EMOJI_FONT`) and `track` (a point, ring or cross that follows a spot of the picture by Lucas-Kanade optical flow, or an anchor of the subject mask, with a label, an emoji and a line); new optional `mask` input (the subject, one mask per frame, from any segmentation node; layers that need it fail with a clear message when it is missing); new animation `float`. Tests: outline around a known rectangle, grid on the background only, tracking, every effect.
- Motion Graphics: a node (`MajoorMotionGraphics`, `core/motion.py`) that draws animated texts, boxes and corner brackets over a video from a JSON list of layers (`text`, `box`, `brackets`; `start` / `end` in seconds or as a percentage of the video; animations none, fade, pop, slide_up / down / left / right, typewriter, glitch, shake, draw; `out: fade`), with Pillow, frame by frame, and FFmpeg pipes (the video is encoded again in H.264 with its own audio, the result is cached by the video and the layers). Out-of-range values are brought into range, an unknown animation becomes `none`, an empty list gives the video back. No other pack and no browser: HyperFrames (HeyGen, HTML + GSAP through headless Chrome) was looked at and is not needed for these; it could be an optional second engine for what Pillow cannot draw. New skill `overlay_reader` (the overlays of a video as these layers; the first version wrote one layer per frame, 54 s, and the output was cut off; with the rule one layer per overlay, 24 frames and a calm sampling it gives 17 layers in 36 s, the words and their order right, the times in steps of half a second, a word typed letter by letter sometimes split into several captions). Remix workflow: the control REDRAW THE TEXT OF THE VIDEO (off by default; the reading and the node run only when it is on, behind a lazy switch); Creative workflows: a MOTION GRAPHICS control for your own layers (`[]` = none). Tests: `tests/integration/test_motion.py` (parsing, a typewriter text in its time with the audio kept, every animation) and three checks in `tests/comfy/check_nodes.py`.
- Remix, several people and outfit checks (tested on the 12.2 s outfit-check edit: lineups of the same woman in five outfits, clones, cut-outs). Findings: with the source frames as H3's reference video, only one person of a lineup was replaced, whatever the pose strength (0.55, 0.72, 0.95 gave the same women), and a `retention_analysis` line saying `<Video 1>: fully_preserved - identity` made it worse; the same shot drawn from the pose map alone (`[reference generation]`, no reference video) at strength 0.95 replaced all five, each in the outfit of its position, but redraws the background (beige instead of white) and loses the text and brackets of the source. So: the skill `shot_reader` gets a `PEOPLE` part (counts the people, says when they are the same person) and a `GROUP: yes | no` line; `remix_shot_prompt` gets the rule SEVERAL PEOPLE (every one of them is `<Subject 1>`, one sentence per position), the rule OUTFITS (when the brief says the outfits come from the shot, `subject_definitions` has no garment of `<Picture 1>` and each position wears its own) and two modules (`reference_mode` for video editing, `not_reference_mode` for reference generation, which never names `<Video 1>` and describes the whole shot); `<Video 1>` is always `partially_preserved`. In the workflow a lazy switch gives H3 the source frames only when the reader says `GROUP: no` (a missing line, which Gemma 4 12B left out in 2 shots of 6, counts as a group), the control POSE ONLY forces the pose-only mode, POSE STRENGTH is 0.9, and the writer samples at 0.5 with a 2048-token limit (at 0.7 and 8096 one prompt ran away into repeated words and H3 drew a face close-up). Result on the 5 deployed shots plus the first: every person replaced, in their own outfits, 0 garbled prompts. The GUIDE note of the Remix workflow has an example WHAT TO REPLACE for an outfit check.
- Second real run of `SECATOR Remix` on a 12.2 s portrait edit (a woman in outfits, text over the shots; 432x768 at 24 fps, REF 1 = the man with the afro; adaptive 0.5, 22 shots, 6 regenerated, about 8 minutes): the subject is replaced with the pose, framing, camera and text of the source kept (the woman in sunglasses and a bomber jacket became the man in a brown suit and tie with the same pose), and the soundtrack of the video is kept. Two things found and fixed: a shot of fewer than 5 frames (the shot list has 3-frame shots at the quick cuts) stopped the whole run, because H3 needs at least 5 frames of a reference video; the block now keeps the original take of a shot under 5 frames (a math node, `KEEP THE SHOT`, joins it to `REPLACE: no`); and the shot ids of the `rhythm_text` (cuts grouped by `min_shot_seconds`) are not those of the shot list, so I had picked shots by the wrong ids at first (Deploy Shots uses the shot list). The default `sensitivity` of the Remix workflow is now 0.5. A shot that is kept costs nothing in H3, but it is still read by the LLM.
- First real run of `SECATOR Remix` (GPU instance on another port, a 19.7 s reference of a racing driver reduced to 768x432 at 24 fps, REF 1 = a man with an afro, round glasses and a brown suit, 5 of its 30 shots regenerated, the others kept with `missing_shot_policy = use_original`; about 12 minutes): detection, energy, reading, hearing, writing, H3, the skip switch, Apply and Shot Assemble all ran and the original soundtrack was kept (19.7 s, audio and video in step). The shot where a smiling man looks at the camera became the man with the afro, round glasses and suit; the two shots without a person (cars) were kept as they were (`REPLACE: no`). It found a bug, fixed: the effects that `energy` writes into the plan of a shot kept their sub-cut weights instead of frames, so Select Shot and Apply Shot Metadata stopped with `KeyError: 'frames'` (`tests/integration/test_energy.py` now runs `describe_shot` on them). It also showed that the detection settings decide the quality: with adaptive 0.1 (30 shots) one shot of 1.25 s held three cuts (a helmet close-up, a hand on a sleeve, a gloved hand), H3 mixed them and left part of the original driver in the take; adaptive 0.5 gives 42 shots, adaptive 0.9 54 shots (5.5 cuts/s, many false ones), content 0.5 43 shots. Raise `sensitivity` when a shot holds several scenes. A take is also as long as H3's frame grid allows (39 frames for a shot of 30), Shot Assemble trims it.

- Location plates (creative): the planner can give every shot a place. Select Shot has a new last output `location` (the sentence, empty when the plan gives none). Measured on the planner (Gemma 4 E4B, 5 seeds, a brief with a rooftop, a cafe and the rooftop again): 5 of 5 plans give 2 places and the same sentence for the rooftop shots; with a free sentence per shot instead of an id the model wrote a `location` in 1 run of 3 and varied the wording. - Energy of the edit (inspired by the way Higgsfield Katana measures a reference, no text copied): the option `energy` of Scene Detect & Split (`off`, `events`, `events_and_camera`, last widget; `core/energy.py`) reads a small gray copy of every frame and finds the flashes (white or black), the shake bursts, the punch-ins, the whips and the slow push of the camera (global motion by optical flow). A flash is not a cut: the cuts a detector puts on its frames are dropped when the picture is the same around it (`Katana_Ref.mp4`: 32 shots, 29 with `events_and_camera`). What a shot did becomes the `fx` of its plan (a transition into the shot, sub-cuts with a transition at each event, a zoom for the push) that Shot Assemble puts on the generated take, which comes back calmer than the footage.
