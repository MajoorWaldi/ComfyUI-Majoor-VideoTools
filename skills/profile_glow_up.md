Camera: handheld and low-key at the start (a walk, a quiet street, a window), then steady, low-angle and orbiting shots after the change, slow motion on the reveal
Art direction: the SAME subject in both halves: every prompt, before and after the change, opens with the same sentence about the person (hair, facial hair, glasses, jewelry, build), word for word, and only then says what the person wears; only the setting, the clothes and the grade change, and only as far as the brief asks for a before and after; faded warm tones before, clean contrast and deep colors after; the change is shown by the transition, never by changing who the subject is
