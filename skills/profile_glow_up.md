---
name: Glow-up / Transformation
kind: profile
description: A before and after of the same subject: both states defined, one turning point, a hero ending.
length: 10 to 15 s
cut_density: medium
transition_intensity: bold
creative_freedom: balanced
reference_fidelity: strict
music: fallback only, the music the user gives or asks for wins: a build into a change, 100 to 128 BPM
narrative_coherence: high
---
Intent: the same subject before and after a change.
Progression: state the before (plain, ordinary) and the after (polished) from the brief at the start; list what may change (clothes, hair, setting, grade, posture) and what stays (face, build, identity); one turning point, then a hero ending.
Camera: matching framing and posture across the change so that it reads; handheld and plain before, steadier after.
Rhythm: a calm first half, a short burst at the change, a slower confident second half.
Effects: a speed ramp, a flash, a whip or a glitch for the turning point only.
Art direction: every prompt opens with the same sentence about the subject, word for word, then says what is worn; faded warm tones before, clean contrast after, unless the brief says otherwise.
