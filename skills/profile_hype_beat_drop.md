---
name: Hype / Beat drop
kind: profile
description: Tension that builds and is released at one real change of the music (or of the action): the densest or most striking moment of the edit.
length: 6 to 12 s
cut_density: high
transition_intensity: bold
creative_freedom: free
reference_fidelity: balanced
music: fallback only, the music the user gives or asks for wins: a hard rhythmic track, 120 to 150 BPM, a clear build and a change
narrative_coherence: low
---
Intent: build tension and release it at one real change: a drop, a hit, a break or a key change of the music when it has one, otherwise the strongest moment of the action.
Progression: a set-up that accelerates, the release as the densest or most striking moment, an aftermath that lets it breathe.
Camera: give every cut a new view; push-ins and whip pans on accents; speed ramps from slow to fast into the release.
Rhythm: cuts follow the pulse; a pause before the release only if the music has one; no forced silence.
Effects: flash, whip, glitch, strobe and shake, only as far as the transition setting allows, kept for the release and a few accents.
Art direction: contrast and saturation follow the brief; no fixed city, palette or time of day.
