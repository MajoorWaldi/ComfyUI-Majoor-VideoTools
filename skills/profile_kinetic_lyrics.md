Intent: the lyrics are the actor. Each word appears when it is sung, on or beside the subject, and the edit breathes with the phrases.
Progression: an opening on the subject, the verse phrase by phrase, a release on the strongest line, an end card with the title.
Camera: slow push-ins on the subject; cut on the end of a phrase, never inside a word.
Rhythm: cuts fall on phrase boundaries; use the template kinetic_lyrics for the words and end_card for the last shot.
Effects: warm glow and a few sparks on the accented words; no transition that hides the text.
Art direction: one palette, high contrast between text and picture so the words stay readable.
