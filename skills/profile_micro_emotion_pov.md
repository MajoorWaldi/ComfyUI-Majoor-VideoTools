Intent: one small feeling that changes, with a visible cause.
Progression: show the trigger (a message, an object, a sound, a person), the first reaction, the change of the feeling and the quiet end state.
Camera: say which one it is: a true point of view (the camera is the eyes of the subject, its hands or a phone enter the frame) or an emotional portrait; keep one place and its geography; a slow push-in or a light handheld sway.
Rhythm: few long takes; cut only where the feeling or the view changes; hold on the end state.
Effects: none beyond hard cuts and, at most, a short soft blur.
Art direction: natural light, soft grain, small true details; the face is the story.
