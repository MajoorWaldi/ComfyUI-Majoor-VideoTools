Intent: images that answer the real music.
Progression: follow the actual structure of the track, supplied or generated (phrase, repeat, change); do not invent a verse, a chorus or a drop that it does not have, above all on a short edit; motifs return with variations.
Camera: mix performance and b-roll with distinct views; push-ins on strong hits; speed ramps into a real change.
Rhythm: cuts on beats and phrase starts; the densest part where the music is densest.
Effects: flash, whip, punch and speed ramps on the strongest accents.
Art direction: color-driven, light that pulses with the music; the stylization follows the brief.
