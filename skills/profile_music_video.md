Intent: images that answer the real music.
Progression: follow only section or phrase boundaries supported by the supplied analysis of the actual track; do not infer a verse, chorus or drop from lyrics, tags or mood. Motifs return with variations.
Camera: mix performance and b-roll with distinct useful views; use a push-in or speed ramp only when a measured accent supports it and it does not interrupt a protected gesture.
Rhythm: narrative clarity and protected actions outrank beat snapping. Use reliable section or phrase boundaries for large changes, measured bars or beats for candidate cuts, and detected transients as optional accents. When structure is uncertain, fall back to existing beat-level timing or the narrative.
Effects: accents are candidates, not obligations. Keep effects sparse and never invent exact hit times from a written brief.
Art direction: color-driven, light that pulses with the music; the stylization follows the brief.
