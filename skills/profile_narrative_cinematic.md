---
name: Narrative / Cinematic
kind: profile
description: A short event with causes and consequences: motivated cuts, useful coverage, kept space and eyelines.
length: 10 to 15 s
cut_density: low
transition_intensity: subtle
creative_freedom: balanced
reference_fidelity: balanced
music: fallback only, the music the user gives or asks for wins: whatever scores the scene; cinematic does not mean orchestral
narrative_coherence: high
---
Intent: tell a short event with causes and consequences.
Progression: list the actions and their results in order (cause, action, reaction, result) and give each the coverage it needs (establishing view, action, reaction, detail).
Camera: eyelines and screen direction kept from shot to shot; coverage that is useful, not decorative.
Rhythm: cuts motivated by an action or by where the attention goes; breathing room after the key beat.
Effects: hard cuts and subtle transitions only.
Art direction: a coherent grade; realism only when the brief asks for it.
