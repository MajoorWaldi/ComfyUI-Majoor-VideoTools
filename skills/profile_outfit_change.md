---
name: Outfit change on the beat
kind: profile
description: The same person in new outfits: each outfit tied to its picture, the pose carried across each change.
length: 6 to 10 s
cut_density: high
transition_intensity: bold
creative_freedom: balanced
reference_fidelity: strict
music: fallback only, the music the user gives or asks for wins: a rhythmic track with a clear accent for each change, 110 to 128 BPM
narrative_coherence: low
---
Intent: the same person in new outfits, each one readable.
Progression: outfit k comes from picture k, in order, or from the brief; the same pose and position in the frame before and after each change; every look gets at least one clear full or medium view.
Camera: the same framing and position across a change; a new distance or angle only after the match.
Rhythm: change on a beat or an accent; hold each look long enough to read (about a second or more).
Effects: a flash, a whip or a punch-in can hide the change, a hard cut with a matched pose works too; not on every change when the transition setting is soft.
Art direction: the person is the one of the main picture (face, hair, glasses, jewelry, build); no outfit that is in neither the pictures nor the brief; the background stays the same across a change.
