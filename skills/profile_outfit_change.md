name: Outfit change on the beat
kind: profile
description: The subject changes outfit in a flash on the beat, once or several times: the same person in new looks.
length: 6 to 10 s
cut_density: high
transition_intensity: bold
creative_freedom: balanced
reference_fidelity: strict
music: a rhythmic pop, house or phonk track, 110 to 128 BPM, a clear kick on every beat and a hit or a riser at each change
narrative_coherence: low
---
Rhythm: shots built on the beat, the outfit changes at a hit through a flash, a whip or a punch-in, the pose carried across the change
Camera: full-body and medium shots with the same framing and the same position in the frame before and after each change so that the swap reads, punch-ins on the hits
Art direction: the person is always the one of the main picture (face, hair, glasses, jewelry, build); each new outfit comes from an extra picture when there is one (name its colors and materials) or from the brief; the background stays the same across a change
