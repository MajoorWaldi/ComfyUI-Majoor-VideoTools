---
name: Product / Advertising
kind: profile
description: A hook that is the problem or the desire, one visual proof of the product per shot, a payoff that answers the hook and room for an end card; bright, premium, the product readable in every shot.
length: 8 to 15 s
cut_density: medium
transition_intensity: moderate
creative_freedom: balanced
reference_fidelity: strict
music: derive genre, groove, sound palette and energy arc from the explicit brief and product identity; no default genre, instrument or tempo
narrative_coherence: high
---
Intent: one promise and one proof of it that the eye can see. The viewer knows within three seconds what the product is for and why they should care, with the sound off and with no text on screen.
Hook (the first 1 to 3 seconds): the problem or the desire the product answers, shown as a body or an object in motion: a hand that fails at something, a surface that is dull, a person who wants what the product gives. Not a packshot, not a logo, not a landscape, not a person posing in a calm place. The product may enter at the end of the hook, never later than the second shot. Test: tell the first frame in one sentence to someone who has not read the brief; if there is no tension or no wish in it, rewrite the hook.
Rhythm: establish the problem or the desire, prove it, resolve it. Every shot has ONE visual proof of ONE distinct property or supported use of the product (what it does, how it feels, how it is made, what it replaces) and says it in its own prompt; a shot that proves nothing new is cut, and three similar packshots are not a narrative. The proof is a body, an object or a surface that changes between the first and the last frame of the shot, never a caption or a word: it must be understood with the sound off. Do not force a usage demonstration or an end card when unrequested.
Plant and payoff: plant early and in passing one concrete detail of the product or of its use (a texture, a gesture, a sound, a color, the moment of opening); the payoff in the last shot answers the hook with that detail at its strongest, and is worth more than the plant. The last image is an action that completes or can no longer be undone (the cap pressed shut, the surface that now shines, the hand that no longer fails), never a pose, a silence or a held packshot. When the edit has an end card, it comes after the payoff and only holds it.
Product readable in every shot: the product, or the part of it that the proof is about, is in the frame and in focus in every shot; its shape, cap, colors, materials and label match its reference picture, and a label or logo is never invented or rewritten in the words of a prompt. A shot may show only a detail (a hand, a texture, a spray, a reflection) when that detail is the proof and the product was shown whole in the shot before or after it.
Camera: choose complementary framing for the information of each beat (context, tactile detail, supported action, hero); preserve screen direction and action states. Do not default every shot to a centered bottle and slow backward move. Close-ups carry the proof, the context shots carry the desire.
Art direction: follow the requested mood, materials, palette and lighting; keep product geometry, cap, colors and label appearance consistent. Preserve identity without copying the reference background or framing. Match adjoining action states; varied framing must remain motivated. A person never replaces the product as the subject: a product reference does not become a person, and a hand appears only when the use needs it.
What renders: a face that reacts, a hand that holds, turns, presses, opens, points or hands over a product, one object moving at a time. What breaks, so does not go into a proof: two bodies in contact, fine hand work (tying, pouring onto a surface, unhooking), a liquid that soaks or a material that melts, hardens or folds inside one shot, legible small print. A change (a stain, a shine, a fill level) is told as the shot before and the shot after, never inside one shot.
Effects: hard cuts and the transitions the setting allows; a flash or a punch-in only on the proof or on the payoff.
