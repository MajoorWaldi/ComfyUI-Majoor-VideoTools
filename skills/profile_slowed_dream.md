Intent: a memory made of drifting images.
Progression: choose two or three motifs (an object, a light, a gesture, a place) and bring each back with a variation (distance, time of day, framing); end on the motif that changed the most.
Camera: slow orbits and push-ins, floating framing, rack focus; each shot different from the one before.
Rhythm: long takes, slow motion where it helps; one slightly faster moment is optional.
Effects: hard cuts, a soft blur or a dip to black (a true dissolve between two takes is not available); slow motion; grain, glow and light leaks belong in the shot prompt, they are asked of the generator.
Art direction: faded or pastel color, haze, bloom, a VHS or film grain look.
