Progression: introduce the presence, bring the signature back with a useful variation each time (angle, distance, light, speed), end on its strongest version; no interchangeable hero poses.
Camera: change the point of view and the distance between repeats; low-angle or hero frames only where they add something; steady holds against a moving surrounding.
Rhythm: short bursts of cuts only on motivated accents, separated by calm holds; not every shot is a burst.

Art direction: bold, high contrast, stylized; raw or lo-fi texture is welcome; the setting follows the brief.
